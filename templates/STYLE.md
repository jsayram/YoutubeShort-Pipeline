# Visual style

## Direction

Describe the visual world in a few concrete sentences. Name the medium, surface, palette, line
quality, typography mood, and the amount of empty space.

## Fixed rules

- Vertical 9:16 composition.
- No logos or watermarks.
- Do not ask the image model to render important text.
- Keep the central subject inside mobile-safe margins.
- Use the same palette and illustration language across every image.
- Reserve one accent color for the closing scene.

## Motion direction

- Opening: quick reveal with one strong camera move.
- Explanation: staged type, diagrams, and state changes.
- Example: prompt-to-result transformation.
- Contrast: split or opposing movement.
- Closing: upward assembly followed by a readable hold.
