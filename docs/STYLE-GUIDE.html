06 · Motion guardrails

Motion explains the story or it does not belong.

Do

  • Build, breathe, and resolve inside every scene.
  • Use different speeds, directions, and eases.
  • Separate camera, entrance, and ambient layers.
  • Pair numbers with bars, rings, paths, or shapes.
  • Hold the CTA long enough to act on it.

Don't

  • Turn the frame into a six-card dashboard.
  • Ask image generation to render exact text or data.
  • Enter every element from below with one ease.
  • Use constant wobble, zoom, or glow.
  • Fade the final frame to black.
